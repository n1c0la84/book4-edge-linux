Thanks! I read the rework on `anatase` (c618779c07, 62cdef6019) against what

Covered by the rework, I'll drop mine once I've run it:
- PD request after a hot plug (this issue): replaced by requested_rdo /
  requested_pdo tracking and completion through the interrupts.
- Passing the contract to the EC (0x90 input current at 95 %, 0x91 watts and
  100 mV): my `samsung-galaxybook-ec` patch did the same; yours via
  `power-supplies` is cleaner.
- Quiescing the controller across suspend (IRQ off, work cancelled, resync
  on resume): close to my experiment for #3.

Not in it yet:
- #6 orientation: `samsung_emuec` still takes it from CC_STATUS bits 7:4,
  which on this unit is the attach type; the orientation is in bits 1:0
  (1 = CC1, 2 = CC2). Patch in #6.
- #3: with the port's driver bound, unplugging the charger during sleep
  reset the machine on resume here; whether your suspend/resume handlers fix

- Waking for a charger plugged into an empty port during sleep (my

- Device tree for the 14": camera + privacy LED (#2), fingerprint reader on

  yet.

Three more 14" device-tree changes I never sent (patches attached / linked):
- Woofers named by side: the dtsi calls the WSA-macro woofer "SpkrLeft" and
  the WSA2 one "SpkrRight"; on the 14" it is the other way round (each
  SoundWire bus drives the woofer and tweeter of one side). The patch
  overrides the two prefixes and the matching audio-routing sinks; only the
  ALSA control names change.
  https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux/blob/main/dts/patches/0005-arm64-dts-qcom-x1e80100-samsung-galaxy-book4-edge-14.patch
- `eusb5_repeater` (PTN3222 at 0x43 on I2C 5) does not exist on the 14": the
  ACPI tables describe nothing there, no PHY references it, only the 0x4f one
  (usb_2, the fingerprint reader) is present.
  https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux/blob/main/dts/patches/0006-arm64-dts-qcom-x1e80100-samsung-galaxy-book4-edge-14.patch
- Sleep power: `VREG_OCTA_VDD` -> `VREG_DISPLAY_VDD` -> `VREG_EDP_3P3` are
  `regulator-always-on`, so the panel chain stays powered in suspend; the
  panel driver already switches `VREG_EDP_3P3` through runtime PM. Dropping
  `regulator-always-on` (keeping `regulator-boot-on`) is one of the changes
  that took s2idle here from ~1.5 W to ~0.7 W (the same fix took a Vivobook
  S15 from 1.43 W to 0.25 W in justririll/x1e-sleep).
  https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux/blob/main/tools/kernel/sleep-backport/0018-arm64-dts-qcom-galaxy-book4-edge-let-the-panel-suppl.patch

And one driver change: refreshing the battery on read (rate-limited),

resets"). Once Linux talks to it over GLINK that may change, so I'd be glad
to measure sleep on a kernel with your SPSS series.
