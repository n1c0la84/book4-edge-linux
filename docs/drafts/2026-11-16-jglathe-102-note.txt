Two findings from the Galaxy Book4 Edge 14" (X1E80100, EL2 and EL1) that
this thread does not have yet, in case they save someone time:

- **Turning AOSD off:** only `{class: aoss_slp, res: sleep, val: disable}`
  works here. The kernel's debugfs `qcom_aoss/prevent_aoss_sleep` sends
  `{class: aoss_slp, res: sleep: enable|disable}` (no `val`), and with
  either value `aosd` still reached 97 % in the next suspend. Table:
  https://github.com/omacom/omarchy/discussions/15860#discussioncomment-19640176
- **The hourly event:** with AOSD off, `cxsd` gains one entry per full hour
  of the AOP clock while `apss` gains none: the Secure Processor (PAS 14)
  wakes CX. After `qcom_scm_pas_shutdown(14)` there were no visits in 273
  minutes. It is not the cause of the hourly AOSD reset though: with it
  stopped and AOSD on, the machine still power-cycled at the 3 h mark.

Same CL5 result here as in section 3 of the first post: at EL2 TrustZone
refused DRIPS 1.63 billion times in one night without CL5 in the cluster
domains.
