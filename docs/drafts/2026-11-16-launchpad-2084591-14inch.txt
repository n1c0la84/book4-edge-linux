Thanks Rafael, and congratulations on the 16". A status from the 14"
(NP940XMA, X1E80100), in case it helps you and other owners.

I run Arch with our own 7.2-based kernel, not Ubuntu, so this is not a test
of your ISO yet; I can boot it from USB on the 14" and report if you want.

What may be new for your build is sleep. On the 14" the laptop reaches full
deep sleep in s2idle (CX collapse and DDR self-refresh for ~100 % of the
time) at about 0.7 W for the whole board; Windows on the same machine
measures 0.11 W. What it needed, with patches and measurements:
https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux (docs/power.md, docs/PLAN.md)

Three things specific to the Book4 Edge that cost me time:
- AOSD (the state below CX collapse) has to stay off: with it the machine
  power-cycles at a full hour of uptime in s2idle, and in "deep" it lost the
  UFS disk on resume. Only the QMP message
  {class: aoss_slp, res: sleep, val: disable} turns it off; the kernel's
  debugfs prevent_aoss_sleep has no effect on this firmware.
- The ADSP "Modern Standby" message (qcom_adsp_pwr_lmts) crashes Samsung's
  ADSP firmware; Samsung disables that interface on Windows too.
- At EL1, s2idle only collapses if the virtual timer is left armed and
  unmasked; "deep" (PSCI SYSTEM_SUSPEND) works without that.
