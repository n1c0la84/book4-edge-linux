**The Samsung drivers.** On the Book4 Edge, battery, charging, keyboard
backlight, fan profiles and the USB-C ports depend on two Samsung-specific
drivers that linux-omarchy doesn't carry, as far as I can see:
`samsung-galaxybook-ec` (embedded controller: battery, AC, charger input
limit, keyboard backlight, profiles) and `samsung-emuec` (the S2MM006
USB-C/PD controllers behind the EC). They're written and maintained by
antheas in Anatase's tree (`anatase-org/patchwork`, branch `anatase`, now
7.3.4), and he reworked both this week: PD negotiation and the input limit
passed to the EC the way Windows' EmuEC driver does it (c618779c07,
62cdef6019). They bind through device-tree nodes he added on top of Maxim
Storetvedt's series (the EC and both S2MM006 controllers in
`x1-samsung-galaxy-book4-edge.dtsi`, with the EC's `power-supplies` pointing
at them), so taking the drivers means taking those nodes too. Without them a
stock install has no battery reading and no charger control on this laptop.

For the 14" I carry a few more device-tree additions, all sent to Anatase:
the front camera (OV02C10) and its privacy LED
(anatase-org/kernel-anatase#2), the two tweeters, i.e. the second WSA8835 on
each speaker bus (#4), the fingerprint reader on usb_2 (#5), and the Type-C
orientation fix in samsung-emuec (#6). Notes and patches:
https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux

If you want to pick the drivers up, antheas is the person to ask; I'm happy to
test whatever you build on the 14".
