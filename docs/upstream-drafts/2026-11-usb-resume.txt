Subject: X1E80100: USB controllers fail to resume from s2idle (QMP combo PHY init timeout, xHCI "HC died")

Hi,

On a Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge 14" (X1E80100) two different USB resume
failures from s2idle show up, both on resume paths. I can test patches.

1) xHCI on usb_2 (a200000.usb, internal fingerprint reader only) dies

On the first resume after boot:

  [48.124932] dwc3-qcom a800000.usb: port-1 HS-PHY not in L2
  [48.125167] dwc3-qcom a600000.usb: port-1 HS-PHY not in L2
  [48.125334] qcom-pcie 1c08000.pcie: Timeout waiting for L2 entry! LTSSM: 0x11
  [48.126670] xhci-hcd xhci-hcd.1.auto: Abort failed to stop command ring: -110
  [48.126819] xhci-hcd xhci-hcd.1.auto: xHCI host controller not responding, assume dead
  [48.126951] xhci-hcd xhci-hcd.1.auto: HC died; cleaning up
  [48.127102] xhci-hcd xhci-hcd.1.auto: Timeout waiting for reset device command
  [48.127222] PM: resume devices took 15.751 seconds
  [48.127904] usb 1-1: USB disconnect, device number 2

The device (1c7a:05a1) never comes back, and every later suspend fails:

  xhci-hcd xhci-hcd.1.auto: PM: dpm_run_callback(): platform_pm_suspend returns -22
  xhci-hcd xhci-hcd.1.auto: PM: failed to suspend async: error -22
  PM: Some devices failed to suspend, or early wake event detected

so the machine retried suspend every ~30 s all night (about 3000
attempts) instead of sleeping. Unbinding and rebinding a200000.usb from
dwc3-qcom recovers it (device back within 1 s, suspend works again).
Seen once so far; normally the device disconnects and re-enumerates on
every resume without errors.

2) QMP combo PHY (fd5000, usb_1 / a600000) init timeout on resume

With a USB 2 hub and a USB stick on that Type-C port:

  qcom-qmp-combo-phy fd5000.phy: phy initialization timed-out
  phy phy-fd5000.phy.5: phy init failed --> -110
  dwc3-qcom a600000.usb: Enabling runtime PM for inactive device with active children
  dwc3-qcom a600000.usb: PM: dpm_run_callback(): dwc3_qcom_pm_resume [dwc3_qcom] returns -110
  dwc3-qcom a600000.usb: PM: failed to resume: error -110
  gcc_usb3_prim_phy_pipe_clk already disabled
  WARNING: drivers/clk/clk.c:1257 at clk_core_disable+0x18c/0x1a8
  Call trace:
   clk_core_disable+0x18c/0x1a8 (P)
   qmp_combo_usb_power_on+0x600/0x618 [phy_qcom_qmp_combo]
   qmp_combo_usb_init+0x68/0xc0 [phy_qcom_qmp_combo]
   phy_init+0x78/0x148
   usb_phy_roothub_init+0x50/0xb0
   usb_phy_roothub_resume+0x98/0xe0
  gcc_usb3_prim_phy_pipe_clk already unprepared
  WARNING: drivers/clk/clk.c:1116 at clk_core_unprepare+0x204/0x250
   (same call trace, qmp_combo_usb_power_on+0x608)
  PM: resume devices took 2.387 seconds

The USB 2 devices re-enumerate and work; SuperSpeed on that port is gone
until reboot. The clk warnings look like qmp_combo_usb_power_on()'s error
path disabling the pipe clock that was never enabled when init times out.

Questions:
- Is the pipe-clock unwind in qmp_combo_usb_power_on() on timeout a known
  issue? Happy to test a fix.
- For (1), is there anything known about xHCI on the multiport/usb_2
  controller not surviving s2idle with CX collapse?

System:
- Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge 14" (NP940XMA), X1E80100, BIOS P00AKY.053.250618
- Kernel: 7.3.4 based on <BASE>, booted at EL2; s2idle reaches CX collapse
- Taint: OE (<list, or "none" after the retest>)
