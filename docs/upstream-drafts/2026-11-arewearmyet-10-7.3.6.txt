I tested `linux-omarchy` `7.3.6-6-omarchy` (with `linux-omarchy-dtbs` `7.3-74`, edge) on the NP940XMA at EL1 using the stock 14" DTB, `qcom_tsens` in the initramfs, `qcom_aoss.prevent_aoss_sleep=1` (confirmed `Y` in `/sys/module/qcom_aoss/parameters/prevent_aoss_sleep`), and `usbcore.autosuspend=-1`, without `earlycon=efifb keep_bootcon`.

It booted cleanly with both ADSP and CDSP running and no `qcom_tsens` or `dpu_encoder` errors. Updates on the three open items from my `7.3-78` report:

### 1. Awake charging actually works (stale sysfs cache)

My earlier `~0.27 A` (`4 W`) observation on `7.3-78` was a misleading sysfs readout, not slow charging. On `7.3.6-6`:
- `samsung-emuec 2-0033` negotiated `PD contract 20000 mV 3250 mA (PDO 5)`.
- Over 10 minutes awake (`09:54`–`10:04`), the battery charged from **88% to 94%** (`2816` → `3008 mAh`, `16.564 V` → `17.156 V`, ~19.4 W net into the battery in CV taper).
- Throughout that 10-minute window, `samsung-galaxybook-battery/current_now` stayed frozen at `0` (and `voltage_now`/`capacity` stayed frozen at the plug-in snapshot) until unplugging/replugging triggered a new EC event (`306000` uA).

`samsung-galaxybook-ec` only updates its battery fields on EC AC/battery IRQs (which fire at attach before the charger ramps up) and never refreshes on sysfs read. Adding a refresh-on-read (with a short cache window) fixes it:
https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux/blob/main/drivers/anatase/patches-galaxybook-ec/0001-samsung-galaxybook-ec-refresh-battery-on-read.patch

### 2. Speakers: both sides work

On `7.3.6-6`, both the left and right speakers play cleanly (just quieter due to the PA Volume `17` cap and the tweeters being intentionally omitted, which also explains the two `UNATTACHED` `00:1` SoundWire devices). So the left-only result from `7.3-78` doesn't reproduce here.

### 3. Sleep (`s2idle`): display wakes now, but UFS disk is lost on resume

Tested `s2idle` unplugged on battery with `qcom_aoss.prevent_aoss_sleep=1` (`Y`):
- Unlike `7.3-78`, the display woke cleanly with no `dpu_encoder_frame_done_timeout` freeze.
- However, the **UFS root disk was lost on resume** (nothing hit disk after `PM: suspend entry (s2idle)`), requiring a reboot.

I ran into the same UFS resume failure earlier on this board when interconnects sync (`state_synced` on `aggre2`, documented in [power.md](https://github.com/n1c0la84/book4-edge-linux/blob/main/docs/power.md#forcing-the-sync-breaks-resume)): on resume, `ufs_qcom` re-initializes `1d80000.phy` at PWM-gear interconnect votes instead of `MODE_MAX`, hits `qcom-qmp-ufs-phy 1d80000.phy: phy initialization timed-out` / `Failed to calibrate PHY: -110`, and drops the disk. Holding an active-only interconnect vote on `/soc@0/ufshc@1d84000` (or voting `MODE_MAX` during `ufs_qcom_power_up_sequence()`) avoided it on my kernel.

Two other things from the `7.3.6-6` log that may be related or useful for sleep power:
- While awake, `linux-omarchy` logs this every ~3–10 s (not seen on my 7.3.4 kernel):
  ```
  ufshcd-qcom 1d84000.ufshc: ufshcd_dev_cmd_completion: unexpected response in Query RSP: fd
  ufshcd-qcom 1d84000.ufshc: ufshcd_query_attr_qword: opcode 0x04 for idn 48 failed, index 0, selector 0, err = -22
  ufshcd-qcom 1d84000.ufshc: ufshcd_set_timestamp_attr: failed to set timestamp -22
  ```
- Once UFS resume works, note that on 7.3 (`8017f8fa34`) CoreSight `ctcu@10001000` keeps `qdss` voted and blocks CX collapse in `s2idle`; disabling `ctcu@10001000` in the 14" DTS restored ~97% CX/DDR residency on my 7.3.4 kernel.

Happy to test any UFS/sleep patch or capture a `pm_test=devices` / `earlycon` log if it helps narrow down the resume failure.
