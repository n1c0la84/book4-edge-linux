Subject: soundwire: qcom: bus clash/clock-stop wedge on X1E80100; controller remove/re-probe bugs

Hi,

On a Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge 14" (X1E80100, four WSA8835 speaker amps
on 6ab0000/6b10000 and WCD9380 on 6ad0000/6d30000) a "bus clash" interrupt
or parity error wedges the affected SoundWire peripherals in UNATTACHED or
Alert until reboot. Investigating recovery and controller rebinding also
showed three separate bugs in qcom_swrm_probe()/qcom_swrm_remove(). Details
below; I can test patches.

1) Bus clash / parity error wedges peripherals (and blocks CX collapse)

Usually shortly after resume from s2idle, or when PipeWire starts a stream
after runtime-PM idle:

  [1065.013053] qcom-soundwire 6b10000.soundwire: qcom_swrm_irq_handler: SWR bus clsh detected
  [1065.021047] qcom-soundwire 6ab0000.soundwire: qcom_swrm_irq_handler: SWR bus clsh detected

All four amps then read UNATTACHED in /sys/bus/soundwire/devices/*/status,
and the next playback fails:

  wsa883x-codec sdw:4:0:0217:0202:00:2: ASoC error (-61): at snd_soc_pcm_component_pm_runtime_get() on sdw:4:0:0217:0202:00:2
   WSA Playback: ASoC error (-61): at __soc_pcm_open() on WSA Playback
   MultiMedia2 Playback: ASoC error (-61): at dpcm_be_dai_startup() on MultiMedia2 Playback
  qcom-soundwire 6ab0000.soundwire: swrm_wait_for_wr_fifo_avail err write overflow
  qcom-soundwire 6ab0000.soundwire: qcom_swrm_irq_handler: SWR unknown interrupt value: 2048

Other occurrences on this machine: 20 and 28 Oct, 23 Nov ("Bus clash
detected", "Reached MAX_RETRY" and parity errors on sdw:1:0:0217:0202:00:{1,2},
both amps stuck in Alert), 24 Nov (clashes on 6ab0000, 6ad0000 and 6b10000;
that time the amps recovered on their own), and 27 Nov (stream start at
11:11:16 triggered `wcd9380-codec sdw:2:0:0217:010d:00:4: Parity error
detected` + `6ad0000.soundwire: SWR bus clsh detected`, leaving sdw:2 stuck in
Alert). While sdw:2 sat in Alert on 27 Nov, a subsequent 50-minute s2idle
sleep reached 0% cxsd/ddr (1.64 W vs ~0.81 W) even though APSS, ADSP and
CDSP slept and all Linux clocks/interconnects/power domains matched normal
99.5% CX-collapse boots.

Downstream (Omarchy on Glymur v3.1.0 controllers), forcing
`ctrl->clock_stop_not_supported = true` so runtime resume soft-resets and
re-enumerates instead of exiting clock-stop has been used to avoid slaves
waking unattached/corrupted from clock-stop.

2) wsa883x cannot be rebound

wsa883x-codec has no bind/unbind attributes (writes fail with EACCES even
as root), so the amps cannot be re-probed on their own.

3) Controller remove/re-probe bugs (three issues in soundwire/qcom.c)

With PipeWire stopped, unbinding 6ab0000.soundwire and 6b10000.soundwire
from qcom-soundwire removed the sound card as expected, but hit three
driver bugs:

a) qcom_swrm_remove() deletes the bus without quiescing the controller IRQ:
   during remove, qcom_swrm_irq_handler() kept enumerating and tried to
   re-add a slave while sdw_bus_master_delete() was tearing the bus down:

     sysfs: cannot create duplicate filename '/devices/platform/soc@0/6b10000.soundwire/sdw-master-1-0/sdw:1:0:0217:0202:00:2'
      sdw_slave_add+0x204/0x2d0 [soundwire_bus]
      qcom_swrm_enumerate.isra.0+0x1c4/0x1e8 [soundwire_qcom]
      qcom_swrm_irq_handler+0x45c/0x580 [soundwire_qcom]
      sdw_bus_master_delete+0x30/0x70 [soundwire_bus]
      qcom_swrm_remove+0x20/0x50 [soundwire_qcom]
     kobject: kobject_add_internal failed for sdw:1:0:0217:0202:00:2 with -EEXIST, ...
     soundwire sdw-master-1-0: Failed to add slave: ret -17

b) qcom_swrm_probe() forces IRQF_TRIGGER_RISING over DT IRQ_TYPE_LEVEL_HIGH:
   hamoa.dtsi (and other Qualcomm DTs) declare these SoundWire interrupts as
   level-high, so probe rewrites the mapping to edge and leaves it that way
   on unbind. Rebinding then fails:

     irq: type mismatch, failed to map hwirq-202 for interrupt-controller@17000000!
     qcom-soundwire 6b10000.soundwire: error -EINVAL: request_irq(0) 0x0 qcom_swrm_irq_handler [soundwire_qcom] soundwire
     qcom-soundwire 6b10000.soundwire: probe with driver qcom-soundwire failed with error -22
     (same for 6ab0000 with hwirq-203)

c) qcom_swrm_remove() does not disable runtime PM or guard hclk against
   already-runtime-suspended controllers, so remove calls
   clk_disable_unprepare(ctrl->hclk) unconditionally and leaves runtime PM
   enabled for a subsequent probe.

(Note: Birk Skyum of Omarchy also hit (b) and (c) on Yoga Slim 7x ADSP
restarts and has downstream patches for those two.)

Questions:
- Is a bus clash / clock-stop exit on these v2.0.0 X1E80100 buses expected
  to be recoverable (bus reset / re-enumeration), or should clock-stop be
  avoided here as on v3.1.0?
- Would you take patches for the three probe/remove fixes (DT IRQ trigger,
  disable_irq() before sdw_bus_master_delete(), and balancing pm_runtime /
  hclk in qcom_swrm_remove())?
