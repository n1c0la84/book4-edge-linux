Subject: soundwire: qcom: bus clash leaves WSA883x amps detached on X1E80100; controller unbind/bind cannot recover

Hi,

On a Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge 14" (X1E80100, four WSA8835 speaker amps
on two SoundWire buses, 6ab0000 and 6b10000) the speakers stop working
after a "bus clash" interrupt, and nothing short of a reboot brings them
back. Trying to recover by unbinding and rebinding the controller exposed
two further problems in soundwire-qcom. Details below; I can test patches.

1) Bus clash leaves the amps unusable

Usually shortly after resume from s2idle, or at the start of playback:

  [1065.013053] qcom-soundwire 6b10000.soundwire: qcom_swrm_irq_handler: SWR bus clsh detected
  [1065.021047] qcom-soundwire 6ab0000.soundwire: qcom_swrm_irq_handler: SWR bus clsh detected

(here 33 s after resume). All four amps then read UNATTACHED in
/sys/bus/soundwire/devices/*/status, and the next playback fails:

  wsa883x-codec sdw:4:0:0217:0202:00:2: ASoC error (-61): at snd_soc_pcm_component_pm_runtime_get() on sdw:4:0:0217:0202:00:2
   WSA Playback: ASoC error (-61): at __soc_pcm_open() on WSA Playback
   MultiMedia2 Playback: ASoC error (-61): at dpcm_be_dai_startup() on MultiMedia2 Playback
  qcom-soundwire 6ab0000.soundwire: swrm_wait_for_wr_fifo_avail err write overflow
  qcom-soundwire 6ab0000.soundwire: qcom_swrm_irq_handler: SWR unknown interrupt value: 2048

Other occurrences on this machine: 20 and 28 Oct, 23 Nov (that time "Bus
clash detected", "Reached MAX_RETRY" and parity errors on
sdw:1:0:0217:0202:00:{1,2}, both amps stuck in "Alert"), 24 Nov (clashes
on 6ab0000, 6ad0000 and 6b10000; that time the amps came back by
themselves). Roughly once a day of use, more often with many suspend
cycles. Restarting PipeWire does not help.

2) wsa883x cannot be rebound

wsa883x-codec has no bind/unbind attributes (writes fail with EACCES even
as root), so the amps cannot be re-probed on their own.

3) Controller unbind races its own IRQ, and re-probe fails

With PipeWire stopped, unbinding 6ab0000.soundwire and 6b10000.soundwire
from qcom-soundwire removed the sound card as expected, but during
qcom_swrm_remove() the IRQ handler was still enumerating and tried to
re-add a peripheral that was being deleted:

  sysfs: cannot create duplicate filename '/devices/platform/soc@0/6b10000.soundwire/sdw-master-1-0/sdw:1:0:0217:0202:00:2'
   sdw_slave_add+0x204/0x2d0 [soundwire_bus]
   qcom_swrm_enumerate.isra.0+0x1c4/0x1e8 [soundwire_qcom]
   qcom_swrm_irq_handler+0x45c/0x580 [soundwire_qcom]
   sdw_bus_master_delete+0x30/0x70 [soundwire_bus]
   qcom_swrm_remove+0x20/0x50 [soundwire_qcom]
  kobject: kobject_add_internal failed for sdw:1:0:0217:0202:00:2 with -EEXIST, ...
  soundwire sdw-master-1-0: Failed to add slave: ret -17

Binding them again then failed for both controllers:

  irq: type mismatch, failed to map hwirq-202 for interrupt-controller@17000000!
  qcom-soundwire 6b10000.soundwire: error -EINVAL: request_irq(0) 0x0 qcom_swrm_irq_handler [soundwire_qcom] soundwire
  qcom-soundwire 6b10000.soundwire: probe with driver qcom-soundwire failed with error -22
  (same for 6ab0000 with hwirq-203)

So the IRQ seems to be left mapped with a different trigger type after
the first probe, and remove does not quiesce the IRQ before deleting the
bus. The full log of both events is available on request.

Questions:
- Is a bus clash on these WSA buses expected to be recoverable in the
  driver (re-enumeration, a bus reset), or is the clash itself the bug
  (e.g. timing/clock-stop around s2idle)?
- Would you take a fix that disables the IRQ before sdw_bus_master_delete()
  in qcom_swrm_remove(), and is the re-probe IRQ type mismatch known?

System:
- Samsung Galaxy Book4 Edge 14" (NP940XMA), X1E80100, BIOS P00AKY.053.250618
